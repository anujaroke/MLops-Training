1. PURPOSE & PHILOSOPHY
────────────────────────
NexusTech Solutions is a hybrid-first company. We believe great work happens when
employees have the autonomy to choose where they work best — while maintaining the
in-person connection that fuels collaboration, culture, and creativity.

This policy is built on three principles:
  TRUST        : We trust employees to deliver results wherever they work
  FLEXIBILITY  : We design work around outcomes, not hours or location
  CONNECTION   : We create intentional in-person moments that matter

This policy replaces all previous remote work arrangements communicated during or after
the COVID-19 pandemic and represents our permanent hybrid framework.

2. WORK ARRANGEMENT TYPES
──────────────────────────

2.1 Hybrid (Default for most roles)
  Definition  : Employee works from a NexusTech office location AND remotely in a
                flexible pattern agreed upon with their manager.
  Expectation : Minimum 2 in-office days per week (Tuesday and Thursday preferred as
                "anchor days" for team collaboration)
  Eligible for: All employees within 50 miles of a NexusTech office

2.2 Remote (Approved exceptions)
  Definition  : Employee works fully remotely with no regular in-office requirement.
  Eligibility : Employees who live more than 50 miles from any NexusTech office OR
                those with an approved Remote Exception (see Section 6)
  In-person requirement: Must attend quarterly in-person team weeks (travel covered by NT)

2.3 Office-First (Required for some roles)
  Definition  : Employee is expected in the office 4–5 days per week.
  Applicable roles:
    - IT Infrastructure / Help Desk technicians (on-site equipment management)
    - Office Operations, Reception, Facilities
    - Lab / Hardware Engineering roles requiring physical equipment
    - New employees during 90-day probationary period (must be in office 3+ days/week)

2.4 Temporary Remote (Short-Term Flexibility)
  Definition  : Employee temporarily works fully remote for up to 4 weeks for approved
                personal reasons (caretaking, home repairs, travel with family, etc.)
  Process     : Verbal/Slack agreement with direct manager; no formal HR approval needed
                for periods up to 2 weeks. Workday submission required for 2–4 weeks.
  Extensions  : Beyond 4 weeks, requires HR Business Partner approval.

3. ELIGIBILITY
───────────────
Remote and hybrid arrangements are available to:
  ✅ Full-time permanent employees who have completed 90-day probation
  ✅ Part-time employees (pro-rated in-office expectations)
  ✅ Long-term contractors (6+ months engagement) with manager approval

NOT eligible for remote/hybrid arrangements:
  ✗ Employees on an active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) — must revert to office-first
    until PIP is successfully completed (exceptions require CPO approval)
  ✗ Employees within 90-day probationary period
  ✗ Employees with a documented pattern of productivity issues tied to remote work

4. IN-OFFICE ANCHOR DAYS
─────────────────────────

4.1 Company Anchor Days
NexusTech designates Tuesday and Thursday as preferred in-office days for most teams.
These days are intentionally chosen to:
  - Maximize the chance that teammates are in-office simultaneously
  - Create a midweek energy boost
  - Enable spontaneous collaboration and social connection

All-hands meetings, team offsites, and department town halls default to anchor days.

4.2 Team-Level Customization
  - Teams may customize anchor days with EM approval, provided the team collectively agrees
  - Cross-functional teams that collaborate heavily should align anchor days
  - Engineering squads should be in-office on the same days for sprint ceremonies

4.3 Anchor Day Activities (Encouraged)
  - Team lunches (NexusTech provides $20/person lunch stipend on Tuesdays)
  - In-person sprint planning, design reviews, architecture discussions
  - 1:1s and performance conversations
  - Social events, happy hours, and team bonding

5. HOME OFFICE SETUP & STIPENDS
─────────────────────────────────

5.1 New Hire Remote Setup Allowance
  All new employees approved for hybrid or remote work receive a one-time home office
  stipend of $1,000 USD (or local currency equivalent) to spend within the first 90 days.

  Approved uses:
    ✓ Monitor (external display)
    ✓ Ergonomic chair
    ✓ Standing desk or desk riser
    ✓ Webcam, microphone, or headset
    ✓ Keyboard and mouse
    ✓ Desk lamp, cable management accessories
    ✓ Wi-Fi extender or ethernet adapter

  NOT covered by stipend:
    ✗ Personal computers or tablets (company equipment is provided separately)
    ✗ Home renovation or construction costs
    ✗ Coffee machines or personal appliances
    ✗ Internet service (see Section 5.3)

  Receipts must be submitted via Workday Expenses within 30 days of purchase.
  Equipment purchased with the stipend remains the employee's personal property.

5.2 Monthly Remote Work Stipend (Ongoing)
  Remote employees (non-hybrid) receive:
    - $100/month USD remote work stipend
    - No receipts required; paid directly via payroll as a taxable benefit

  Hybrid employees receive:
    - $50/month hybrid stipend (to cover incidental supplies, printing, etc.)
    - No receipts required

5.3 Internet & Phone
  - NexusTech does NOT reimburse home internet costs (considered a personal utility).
  - Employees who require dedicated high-speed internet for specific job roles
    (e.g., video production, live streaming support) may request a monthly internet
    reimbursement of up to $60/month with manager and Finance approval.
  - Company mobile plans are available for roles requiring constant mobile access
    (account managers, executive team, on-call engineers). Contact IT.

5.4 Company-Issued Equipment
  Standard equipment issued to all remote/hybrid employees:
    - MacBook Pro (14" or 16" depending on role) or equivalent Windows machine
    - NexusTech-branded laptop bag
    - Power adapter + travel charger
    - YubiKey security key
    - NexusTech swag kit (first week)

  Requesting additional equipment (docking station, second monitor, mechanical keyboard, etc.):
    Submit an IT equipment request via the IT Service Portal (it.nexustech.io).
    Standard requests fulfilled within 5 business days.
    Specialty equipment (e.g., GPU workstation, large format display) requires manager + IT approval.

6. REMOTE EXCEPTION PROCESS
─────────────────────────────

6.1 When a Remote Exception is Needed
  Employees who want to work fully remote from a location beyond commuting distance of a
  NexusTech office must submit a Remote Work Exception Request.

  Common scenarios:
    - Relocating to a city without a NexusTech office
    - Personal circumstances requiring a change in work location
    - New hire who is being hired into a fully remote role

6.2 Approval Process
  Step 1 : Employee submits Remote Exception Form in Workday
           (include: proposed location, reason, start date, duration — temporary or permanent)
  Step 2 : Direct manager reviews and provides recommendation
  Step 3 : HR Business Partner reviews for compliance (tax, labor law, entity requirements)
  Step 4 : Finance reviews compensation implications (geographic pay adjustments if applicable)
  Step 5 : CPO provides final approval for permanent remote exceptions
  Step 6 : Employee notified within 15 business days of submission

6.3 Geographic Pay Adjustments
  NexusTech uses a location-based compensation model for remote employees:

  Tier 1 (High Cost of Living) — San Francisco, New York, Seattle, San Jose:
    Compensation at 100% of published band

  Tier 2 (Mid Cost of Living) — Austin, Denver, Boston, Chicago, Los Angeles, Portland:
    Compensation at 90–95% of published band

  Tier 3 (Lower Cost of Living) — All other US locations:
    Compensation at 80–90% of published band

  International remote (approved countries): Compensation benchmarked to local market data.
  Relocation that results in a geographic downward adjustment will be applied at next
  compensation cycle (not retroactively mid-year).

7. INTERNATIONAL REMOTE WORK
──────────────────────────────

7.1 Working from Another Country Temporarily ("Workcation")
  Employees may work from a country other than their home country for up to 30 consecutive
  days per calendar year without triggering tax or employment law concerns, subject to:
    - Manager approval via Workday (at least 5 business days in advance)
    - Must maintain work hours that overlap with team's core hours by at least 4 hours/day
    - Responsible for any personal visa requirements (NexusTech does not arrange visas)
    - Travel insurance must be in place (provided by NexusTech through Cigna Global)

  Countries requiring additional legal review (do NOT work from without HR approval):
    China, Russia, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Venezuela (entity/sanctions concerns)

7.2 Permanent International Remote
  - Extremely limited; requires CPO + CFO + General Counsel approval
  - NexusTech must have a legal entity or employer-of-record (EOR) arrangement in that country
  - Currently approved for: USA, India, Singapore, UK, Canada, Germany, Australia
  - New country requests reviewed case-by-case; typical setup time: 3–6 months

8. WORKING HOURS & AVAILABILITY
─────────────────────────────────

8.1 Core Hours
  All employees must be available (reachable via Slack) during core hours:
    US Teams     : 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM in the employee's local US time zone
    India Team   : 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM IST
    APAC Team    : 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM SGT

  Outside core hours, employees may flex their schedules to their preference.

8.2 Working Hours Flexibility
  - Employees may start as early as 6:00 AM or as late as 10:00 AM local time
  - Standard working day is 8 hours (not including breaks)
  - Compressed workweek (4x10) requires manager approval and may not be compatible with
    all roles
  - Part-time schedules must be agreed in the employment contract and cannot be
    unilaterally changed by the manager

8.3 Availability & Response Expectations
  Slack:
    - During core hours: Respond within 1 hour to direct messages
    - Non-urgent messages outside core hours: Next business day
    - Set your Slack status to reflect your location (🏠 WFH / 🏢 In Office / ✈️ Traveling)
    - Use Do Not Disturb for deep focus blocks (notify manager if extended)

  Email:
    - Not expected for real-time communication
    - Respond within 24 hours during business days

  Video calls:
    - Camera on is the default expectation (unless in a noisy/public location)
    - Camera-off is always acceptable for audio-only preference or personal circumstances —
      employees should never feel pressured to have camera on

8.4 Disconnecting After Hours
  - Employees are NOT expected to respond to Slack or email outside working hours
  - Managers must not contact employees outside working hours via personal numbers unless
    there is a genuine emergency (production outage, time-sensitive safety issue)
  - NexusTech discourages a culture of always-on messaging — use scheduled send for
    messages composed outside business hours

9. MEETING STANDARDS FOR HYBRID TEAMS
───────────────────────────────────────

9.1 Remote-First Meeting Principles
  Even when some participants are in the office, all meetings are conducted as if everyone
  is remote to ensure an equitable experience:
    - Every participant joins via their own laptop (no "in-room + dial-in" split)
    - Meeting rooms are equipped with high-quality cameras and microphones
    - Shared docs / screen share is the norm for presentations
    - Chat is monitored and questions from remote participants are prioritized

9.2 Meeting Norms
  - Meetings start on time; late starts waste everyone's time
  - Meetings have an agenda shared at least 30 minutes in advance
  - Default meeting length: 25 min (half-hour slot) or 50 min (hour slot)
    — avoids back-to-back meeting fatigue; leaves buffer for bio breaks
  - Fridays are protected: no internal meetings after 3 PM local time (deep focus window)
  - "No Meeting" blocks: Employees may block 2 hours of uninterrupted deep work per day
    — mark as "Focus Time" in Google Calendar; others must respect this

9.3 Meeting-Free Days
  Wednesday is a company-wide low-meeting day. Guidelines:
    - No mandatory all-hands, town halls, or large group meetings on Wednesdays
    - 1:1s and small working sessions are acceptable
    - Encouraged for deep focus, independent work, learning, writing

10. HOME OFFICE SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
───────────────────────────────────────
(In conjunction with NT-IT-002 Information Security Policy)

  - Home router must use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption with a non-default password
  - VPN (Cisco AnyConnect) must be active when accessing company systems
  - Company laptop must not be used by family members or shared with anyone
  - Screen must not be visible to others in public or shared spaces when working with
    Tier 3/4 data (use a privacy screen filter — available from IT at no cost)
  - Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 1 hour: it@nexustech.io / 1100

11. MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES
─────────────────────────────
Managers of hybrid/remote employees must:
  ✓ Set clear expectations for availability and deliverables in writing
  ✓ Conduct weekly or biweekly 1:1s via video (not just async)
  ✓ Ensure remote employees are not left out of informal decision-making
  ✓ Rotate in-person meeting responsibilities (don't always assign travel to the same person)
  ✓ Proactively check in on remote employee wellbeing and isolation
  ✓ Use Workday to track formal remote arrangements (not just informal agreements)
  ✗ NOT monitor time spent at desk or use screen-monitoring software
  ✗ NOT require employees to be on video continuously or use productivity surveillance tools

12. POLICY VIOLATIONS
──────────────────────
  - Working from an unauthorized country without approval: Written warning + potential
    tax/legal liability investigation
  - Sharing company equipment with unauthorized users: IT security review + disciplinary action
  - Consistently failing to maintain core-hour availability without communication:
    Performance discussion with manager; may affect remote work eligibility
  - Using remote work arrangement to conduct a second job (moonlighting) in violation of
    the NexusTech Conflict of Interest Policy: Termination
