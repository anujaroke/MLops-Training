WELCOME TO NEXUSTECH SOLUTIONS!
---------------------------------
We're thrilled to have you join us. This checklist is your roadmap for the
first 90 days at NexusTech. It is divided into tasks for HR, IT, your Manager,
your Onboarding Buddy, and YOU. Please read every section carefully and check
items off as you complete them in the Darwinbox onboarding portal.

Questions? Contact: hr@nexustech.in | Phone: Ext. 1001
HRMS Portal: portal.nexustech.in
IT Helpdesk: helpdesk.nexustech.in

================================================================================
PRE-JOINING (Done before or on Day 1 by HR & IT)
================================================================================

HUMAN RESOURCES — PRE-JOINING TASKS
  [ ] Offer letter sent and digitally signed via Darwinbox
  [ ] Background verification initiated (via AuthBridge)
  [ ] Employment contract signed (NDA, IP Assignment, Code of Conduct)
  [ ] Welcome email sent with joining instructions and agenda
  [ ] Employee profile created in Darwinbox HRMS
  [ ] Employee added to payroll (Zoho Payroll)
  [ ] Bank account details collected for salary processing
  [ ] PAN card, Aadhaar, and other statutory documents collected
  [ ] PF (Provident Fund) nomination form collected
  [ ] Medical insurance enrollment form sent (insurer: HDFC ERGO Group Policy)
  [ ] ID card photo collected (for access badge)
  [ ] Emergency contact information collected
  [ ] Dietary preferences collected (for welcome lunch)
  [ ] Buddy assigned (from same team; ideally 1+ year at NexusTech)
  [ ] Manager notified of joining date, role, and team allocation

IT — PRE-JOINING SETUP
  [ ] Employee email account created (Google Workspace):
      Format: firstname.lastname@nexustech.in
  [ ] SSO account provisioned (Okta)
  [ ] Role-based access groups assigned in IAM (based on department, role)
  [ ] Laptop imaged and prepared:
        - OS: macOS 14 Sonoma (engineers) / Windows 11 Pro (others)
        - Crowdstrike EDR agent installed
        - Jamf / Intune MDM enrolled
        - VPN client (WireGuard) installed
        - Standard app bundle pre-installed (see IT Onboarding SOP)
  [ ] Corporate email signature template applied
  [ ] Hardware accessories packed (cable, charger, mouse, keyboard if needed)
  [ ] YubiKey provisioned (engineers and Grade 3+)
  [ ] Access badge printed and ready for collection at reception
  [ ] Welcome IT kit prepared (includes: laptop, accessories, NexusTech swag bag)

MANAGER — PRE-JOINING TASKS
  [ ] 30-60-90 day plan drafted and ready to share on Day 1
  [ ] Team introduction email drafted
  [ ] First-week meeting schedule planned
  [ ] First project or assignment identified (not overwhelming; structured)
  [ ] Access rights request submitted to IT (any special access needed)
  [ ] Desk / workspace prepared (for in-office employees)
  [ ] Introductory 1:1 scheduled for Day 1 (90 minutes)

================================================================================
DAY 1 CHECKLIST
================================================================================

ARRIVAL & ADMIN (9:00 – 10:00 AM)
  [ ] Greeted by HR or Onboarding Buddy at reception
  [ ] Office tour: workstation, break room, restrooms, meeting rooms, exits
  [ ] Access badge collected and tested
  [ ] Seat assignment confirmed
  [ ] Emergency exits and safety procedures briefed
  [ ] Signed physical access agreement

IT SETUP (10:00 AM – 12:00 PM) — with IT Helpdesk support
  [ ] Laptop collected and powered on
  [ ] SSO login configured (Okta) with MFA setup
  [ ] Corporate email (Gmail) verified and working
  [ ] Slack workspace joined: nexustech.slack.com
      Join channels: #general, #[team-channel], #announcements,
      #it-helpdesk, #engineering (if applicable), #random
  [ ] 1Password Teams account set up; emergency kit saved securely
  [ ] YubiKey registered with SSO (engineers)
  [ ] VPN client tested (connect to corporate network)
  [ ] MacOS/Windows initial update run; all MDM profiles confirmed
  [ ] GitHub Enterprise access verified (engineers)
  [ ] Jira / Confluence access verified
  [ ] Zoom / Google Meet tested (camera, microphone, speaker)
  [ ] Expensify account linked
  [ ] Darwinbox profile completed (photo, personal details, leave preferences)

HR ONBOARDING SESSION (1:00 – 2:30 PM)
  [ ] Welcome address from HR (Sarah Mitchell or delegate)
  [ ] Company history, mission, and values presentation
  [ ] Org structure walkthrough
  [ ] Key policies reviewed:
      - Leave Policy (HR-POL-001)
      - Code of Conduct
      - IT Security Policy (IT-SEC-002)
      - Anti-Harassment Policy
      - Expense Policy (FIN-POL-004)
  [ ] Benefits overview:
      - Health insurance (self + dependents)
      - PF and Gratuity
      - NPS (optional)
      - Flexi-benefit allowance
  [ ] Payroll cycle explained (salary on 28th of each month)
  [ ] Performance review cycle explained
  [ ] Darwinbox navigation walkthrough

MANAGER 1:1 — DAY 1 (3:00 – 4:30 PM)
  [ ] Personal introduction and team dynamics discussion
  [ ] Role expectations and first 30-day goals shared
  [ ] 30-60-90 day plan reviewed and handed over
  [ ] Introduction to key stakeholders and points of contact
  [ ] Regular 1:1 cadence established (weekly recommended)
  [ ] First assignment briefed

BUDDY INTRODUCTION (4:30 – 5:00 PM)
  [ ] Buddy meets new hire
  [ ] Buddy contact details exchanged
  [ ] Buddy availability and communication preference agreed
  [ ] Buddy introduces to the broader team informally

WELCOME LUNCH / TEAM GATHERING
  [ ] Team welcome lunch arranged by manager (if not Day 1, within first 3 days)

================================================================================
WEEK 1 CHECKLIST (Days 1–5)
================================================================================

COMPANY & PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE
  [ ] Read company overview deck (Google Drive: Company Intro 2026)
  [ ] Watch CEO "State of NexusTech" Town Hall recording (latest: Jan 2026)
  [ ] Review current product portfolio:
      - NexusTech FinOS product overview
      - NexusTech LogiTrack product overview
      - NexusTech DataPulse product overview
  [ ] Review Company OKRs for 2026 (Notion OKR Workspace — read access)
  [ ] Complete "NexusTech 101" course in Darwinbox LMS (2 hours; mandatory)

SECURITY & COMPLIANCE TRAINING (Mandatory — complete within first 7 days)
  [ ] Annual Security Awareness Training (IT-SEC-002 referenced)
      — Available in Darwinbox LMS → Security → Annual Awareness Training
      — Duration: ~2 hours; includes a short quiz (must score ≥80% to pass)
  [ ] Acknowledge IT Security Policy (digital acknowledgment in Darwinbox)
  [ ] Acknowledge Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy
  [ ] POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) online training — complete quiz
  [ ] Acknowledge Data Classification and Handling guidelines

ROLE-SPECIFIC ORIENTATION
  [ ] Meet with team members (10–15 min intro calls with each team member)
  [ ] Attend first team standup / sprint ceremony (as observer)
  [ ] Access and read team's Confluence documentation home page
  [ ] Review team's Jira backlog (read-only, understand project landscape)
  [ ] Review architecture diagrams for product/systems you'll work on
  [ ] Identify 2–3 open tickets appropriate for a first contribution
  [ ] Shadow a colleague on a customer call or stakeholder meeting (if applicable)

FOR ENGINEERS — ADDITIONAL WEEK 1 TASKS
  [ ] Read Engineering_SOP_Code_Review (ENG-SOP-003)
  [ ] Clone the primary repositories from GitHub Enterprise
  [ ] Set up local dev environment (README + setup script in each repo)
  [ ] Run the application locally; access staging environment
  [ ] Complete "Secure Coding Fundamentals" training (Darwinbox LMS, 1.5 hours)
  [ ] Review the Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) folder in Confluence
  [ ] Attend Engineering All-Hands (next scheduled occurrence)
  [ ] Submit first PR (a "hello world" or documentation fix PR to get familiar
      with the GitHub workflow, CI pipeline, and review process)

FOR SALES/CS — ADDITIONAL WEEK 1 TASKS
  [ ] CRM access (Salesforce) configured and tested
  [ ] Review top 5 customer accounts (with manager)
  [ ] Listen to 3 recorded sales/CS calls in Gong
  [ ] Read most recent Sales Playbook (Google Drive: Sales > Playbooks)
  [ ] Complete "NexusTech Product Demo" certification track in Darwinbox LMS

================================================================================
FIRST 30 DAYS CHECKLIST
================================================================================

KNOWLEDGE & RAMP-UP
  [ ] Deep-dive on your primary product/domain (schedule sessions with PMs/leads)
  [ ] Understand the competitive landscape (Marketing: competitive battle cards)
  [ ] Complete all mandatory training modules in Darwinbox LMS
  [ ] Have skip-level 1:1 with manager's manager (introductory; 30 min)
  [ ] Meet cross-functional stakeholders (Product, Design, QA, CS as applicable)

FIRST CONTRIBUTION
  [ ] Complete first meaningful deliverable as agreed in 30-day plan
      (Engineers: merged PR; Sales: shadowed 5 calls; Marketing: first draft)
  [ ] 30-day check-in with manager (structured review of first month)
  [ ] Share early feedback with manager and HR on onboarding experience
      (Darwinbox: Onboarding Feedback Form — Week 4)

OPERATIONAL SETUP
  [ ] Set up filters and labels in Gmail for key communication streams
  [ ] Expense and travel policy read and acknowledged
  [ ] Remote work setup confirmed (if applicable): see Remote_Work_Policy doc
  [ ] LinkedIn profile updated to reflect NexusTech role
  [ ] Personal development goals documented in Darwinbox (will feed into
      mid-year review)

================================================================================
FIRST 60 DAYS CHECKLIST
================================================================================

  [ ] Independently working on assigned projects with moderate guidance
  [ ] Familiarity with team processes, tools, and communication norms
  [ ] At least 1 meaningful contribution to team knowledge base (Confluence
      doc, runbook update, or wiki improvement)
  [ ] 60-day 1:1 with manager: review of progress, feedback, adjustments
  [ ] Peer feedback collected (360-degree informal feedback)
  [ ] Buddy relationship evolving to peer relationship
  [ ] Awareness of Q2/Q3 team goals and how your work contributes

FOR ENGINEERS:
  [ ] Independently opened and closed ≥5 PRs (with reviews completed)
  [ ] Comfortable with on-call rotation expectations (discuss with manager)
  [ ] Architecture of primary system understood well enough to explain to others

================================================================================
FIRST 90 DAYS CHECKLIST
================================================================================

  [ ] Fully independent on core responsibilities; minimal guidance needed
  [ ] 90-day formal review with manager:
        - Performance assessment vs 30-60-90 plan
        - Areas of strength highlighted
        - Growth areas identified with development plan
        - Probation review (if applicable — first 3 months is probation period)
  [ ] Probation confirmation letter issued by HR (or extension communicated)
  [ ] Enrolled in benefits: health insurance dependents, PF nominations confirmed
  [ ] OKR contribution clear for the current quarter
  [ ] Comfortable with all tools, processes, and team norms
  [ ] Given feedback to improve the onboarding process itself (HR survey)
