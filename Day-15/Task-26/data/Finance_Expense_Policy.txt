1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

1.1 Purpose
This policy governs the reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred
by NexusTech Solutions employees in the course of performing their duties. It
ensures fair treatment, fiscal responsibility, and audit compliance.

1.2 Scope
Applies to all permanent employees, contract staff (where explicitly stated in
their contract), and interns (with pre-approved limits). Contractors billing via
invoice are governed by their separate agreements.

================================================================================
2. GENERAL PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

  - Spend company money as you would spend your own — thoughtfully and wisely.
  - When in doubt, ask your manager before incurring an expense.
  - All expenses must be legitimate, business-necessary, and reasonable.
  - Original receipts are required for all claims above INR 200.
  - Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred. Late
    submissions require Finance Director approval; submissions >90 days old
    will not be reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances.
  - No expense may be split across multiple submissions to circumvent limits.
  - Employees must not approve their own expenses.
  - Managers are equally accountable for the expenses they approve.

3. EXPENSE CATEGORIES & LIMITS
================================================================================

3.1 BUSINESS TRAVEL — DOMESTIC
--------------------------------
Applicable for travel within India for meetings, client visits, conferences,
or inter-office visits when more than 50 km from the employee's base location.

Mode of Travel — Economy Class (mandatory for all domestic travel):

  By Air:
    - Book via the approved travel portal (MakeMyTrip Corporate) at least
      7 days in advance to access negotiated fares.
    - Advance booking discount savings must not be forfeited without reason.
    - Business class is not permitted for domestic travel.
    - Seat upgrades at personal cost are the employee's prerogative.

  By Train:
    - AC 1st Class (1A) for overnight journeys (>4 hours).
    - AC 2nd Class (2A) for shorter journeys.
    - Tatkal bookings require manager pre-approval.

  By Road (Long Distance):
    - Cab/taxi from the approved vendor list only (Ola Corporate, Uber
      Business, or company cab pool).
    - Personal vehicle use: Reimbursed at INR 7/km for four-wheelers,
      INR 4/km for two-wheelers. Fuel receipts not required for mileage
      reimbursement but odometer readings must be noted.

  Travel Grades & Per-Diem (Domestic):
  Grade         | Role Level            | Daily Per-Diem (Meals+Incidental)
  --------------|----------------------|-----------------------------------
  Grade 1       | C-Suite / VP         | INR 2,500/day
  Grade 2       | Senior Manager / Lead| INR 1,800/day
  Grade 3       | Manager / Senior Eng | INR 1,400/day
  Grade 4       | Engineer / Analyst   | INR 1,000/day
  Grade 5       | Intern / Junior      | INR 700/day

  Per-diem covers meals and incidentals; hotel is reimbursed separately.

3.2 BUSINESS TRAVEL — INTERNATIONAL
--------------------------------------
Pre-approval required from: Department Head + CFO for travel costs > USD 3,000.

By Air:
  - Economy class mandatory for flights < 8 hours.
  - Premium Economy permitted for flights ≥ 8 hours.
  - Business class: Permitted for C-Suite and VP levels only, or for flights
    ≥ 12 hours for Grade 2 and above with CFO approval.
  - Booking must be done via the corporate travel desk at least 14 days in
    advance.
  - Unused tickets: Cancellations must be reported to travel desk immediately
    to recover refunds.

International Per-Diem (USD per day, all inclusive — meals + incidentals):
  Destination       | Grade 1 | Grade 2 | Grade 3 | Grade 4
  ------------------|---------|---------|---------|--------
  USA/Canada        | $200    | $160    | $130    | $100
  UK/Western Europe | $180    | $150    | $120    | $95
  Southeast Asia    | $120    | $100    | $85     | $70
  Middle East       | $140    | $115    | $95     | $75
  Australia/NZ      | $160    | $135    | $110    | $85

  Per-diem is reduced by 25% for the departure and arrival day.
  Per-diem covers meals and incidentals; hotel is reimbursed separately.

Visa & Travel Insurance:
  - Visa fees are reimbursed 100% with official receipts.
  - NexusTech provides corporate travel insurance for all international
    trips. Employees must register the trip with the travel desk to activate
    coverage.

3.4 MEALS & ENTERTAINMENT
---------------------------
Meals while traveling are covered under per-diem (see 3.1 and 3.2).

Working meals in home city (team lunches, working dinner):
  - Must have a clear business purpose.
  - Minimum 3 employees to qualify as a team meal.
  - Limit: INR 500/person per meal.
  - The most senior person at the meal must submit the expense.

3.5 CLIENT ENTERTAINMENT
--------------------------
Covers meals, events, or outings with customers, prospects, or partners.
  - Pre-approval from manager required for expenses > INR 5,000.
  - Must document: client name, company, business purpose, attendees.
  - Alcohol: Moderate alcohol included in client entertainment (at manager
    discretion); receipt must not itemize alcohol separately (use meal total).
  - Gifts to clients: Up to INR 3,000 per client per year (branded merchandise
    only from the company store); cash gifts prohibited.
  - Per-person limit for client entertainment: INR 3,000/person for meals;
    INR 8,000/person for events (sports, concerts).

3.6 LOCAL TRANSPORTATION
--------------------------
Daily office commute is NOT reimbursable.
Reimbursable local transport scenarios:
  - Client/customer site visit from office: Actual fare, cab receipt required.
  - Airport/station to office or hotel: Actual fare, receipt required.
  - Late-night office departure (after 10 PM): Cab fare reimbursed up to
    INR 600 per instance (request must be pre-declared to manager on the day).

Preferred cab vendors: Ola Corporate, Uber Business, company cab empaneled
vendors. Personal vehicles: INR 7/km (4-wheeler), INR 4/km (2-wheeler).

3.7 COMMUNICATION & INTERNET
------------------------------
Mobile/Data:
  - Company SIM holders: Bill directly to company (standard plan covered).
  - Non-company SIM: Monthly mobile reimbursement:
      Engineers & above: INR 1,000/month
      Sales & field staff: INR 1,500/month
      Others            : INR 600/month
  - International roaming: Actual cost up to USD 50/day (bill to corporate
    travel account).

Internet (Remote Work):
  - Home broadband for remote workers: Up to INR 1,200/month reimbursed.
  - Submit ISP bill or screenshot of payment monthly.

3.8 OFFICE SUPPLIES & EQUIPMENT
---------------------------------
  - Office supplies (pens, notebooks, etc.): Up to INR 500/month without
    prior approval; submit receipt.
  - Equipment for home office (remote workers): Covered under Remote Work
    Policy (see Remote_Work_Policy document) — different approval process.
  - Software subscriptions needed for work: Must be requested via IT portal;
    do not purchase personal licenses and seek reimbursement unless pre-
    approved (it bypasses IT license tracking).

3.9 TRAINING & CONFERENCE
---------------------------
  - External training, courses, certifications: Covered under the Learning &
    Development budget (separate from expense reimbursement). Submit via
    Training Request form in Darwinbox.
  - Conference registration fees: Covered if conference is pre-approved by
    manager and department head. Include agenda/description with claim.
  - Professional memberships (IEEE, ACM, etc.): Up to INR 5,000/year per
    employee with manager approval.

3.10 TEAM EVENTS & OFFSITES
-----------------------------
  - Team lunches/dinners (quarterly): Budget INR 600/person, approved by
    manager.
  - Offsites: Budget and venue require VP and Finance approval.
    Budgeting guidelines: INR 8,000–15,000/person per day for domestic
    offsites (inclusive of stay, meals, and activities).
  - Team celebration for milestones, product launches: Up to INR 500/person
    from the team engagement budget; pre-approved by manager.

4. EXPENSE SUBMISSION PROCESS
================================================================================

Step 1 — Collect Receipts:
  Keep all receipts. Digital receipts (email, WhatsApp) are acceptable.
  For expenses > INR 500, original receipts preferred; scanned copies accepted.

Step 2 — Submit via Expensify:
  Log in to Expensify (linked via SSO at expensify.nexustech.in).
  Create an Expense Report for each trip or month.
  Upload receipts, categorize expenses, add business purpose notes.
  Link to relevant Jira/project ticket if expense is project-specific.

Step 3 — Manager Approval:
  Manager reviews and approves in Expensify within 3 business days.
  Manager may reject with reason; employee must address and resubmit.

Step 4 — Finance Review:
  Finance team audits randomly and reviews all reports > INR 20,000.
  Finance may request additional documentation.

Step 5 — Reimbursement:
  Approved expenses are reimbursed via direct bank transfer in the next
  payroll cycle or within 7 business days for urgent requests.

6. REIMBURSEMENT TIMELINE
================================================================================

Standard submission: Reimbursed in the next monthly payroll (processed on the
28th of each month).
Expense report submission deadline for current month payroll: 20th of the month.
Urgent reimbursement (> INR 10,000 with manager approval): Within 7 business
days via ad-hoc bank transfer.

7. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
================================================================================

The following expenses will NOT be reimbursed under any circumstances:

  - Daily office commute (home to office and back)
  - Traffic fines, parking tickets, or speeding fines
  - Personal clothing, toiletries, or grooming (even during business travel)
  - Alcohol purchased for personal consumption (not as part of client
    entertainment)
  - Tobacco products
  - Personal entertainment (movies, personal streaming subscriptions,
    amusement parks) during travel
  - Hotel minibar, gym, spa, laundry (beyond 5-day trips)
  - Expenses for non-employees (family, friends) accompanying on business trips
  - Gambling or lottery
  - Political donations
  - Personal insurance or medical expenses (covered by company health insurance
    separately)
  - Gym memberships or fitness equipment
  - Expenses for which no receipt exists (for amounts > INR 200)
  - Late submission penalties (airfare change fees incurred due to employee
    negligence)

8. CORPORATE CREDIT CARD POLICY
================================================================================

Corporate credit cards (HDFC Corporate Card) are issued to:
  - All employees at Grade 2 and above
  - Employees with frequent travel requirements (>4 trips/year) at any grade

Card Usage:
  - Must be used for eligible business expenses only.
  - Receipts must still be uploaded to Expensify within 5 days of the charge.
  - Personal charges on corporate card must be reimbursed to the company
    within 5 business days.
  - Credit limit: INR 1,00,000 (Grade 4); INR 2,00,000 (Grade 3); 
    INR 5,00,000 (Grade 2 and above).

Card Loss: Report immediately to Finance (finance@nexustech.in) and the bank.
Card is deactivated within 2 hours of report.

================================================================================
9. ADVANCES
================================================================================

Travel advances up to 80% of estimated trip cost may be requested for domestic
travel. 100% advance for international trips.
Submit Advance Request in Expensify at least 5 business days before travel.
Unused advance must be refunded within 5 business days of returning.
Outstanding advance blocks future advance requests and delays increments if
not settled for >30 days.

================================================================================
10. INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL CURRENCY GUIDELINES
================================================================================

  - Use Forex card (company-arranged via HDFC Forex) for international trips.
  - Cash carry limit: As per RBI limits (currently USD 10,000 equivalent).
  - All international expenses submitted in local currency; Finance converts
    at the official SBI TT buying rate on the date of the expense.
  - Foreign exchange loss/gain due to delay in submission is borne by the
    employee.

================================================================================
11. AUDIT & COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

Finance conducts a 100% audit of all expenses exceeding INR 50,000 and a 10%
random audit of all other expenses.
Expense data is retained for 7 years per financial regulations.
Employees selected for audit must provide original receipts and supporting
context within 5 business days.
All expense reports are subject to review during statutory audits.

13. FAQs
================================================================================

Q: Can I book hotels on personal loyalty accounts?
A: Yes. Keep all loyalty points earned on business travel; this is a personal
   benefit. Just ensure the hotel and rate comply with policy.

Q: What if the approved vendor doesn't have availability?
A: Document the unavailability (screenshot) and book the next best option;
   add a note in your expense report.

Q: Can I claim expenses in cash if POS is unavailable?
A: For amounts up to INR 500, a self-declaration with the purpose is accepted.
   Above INR 500, a handwritten receipt from the vendor (with their name and
   signature) is required.

Q: Who do I contact for Expensify technical issues?
A: Contact it-helpdesk@nexustech.in or raise a ticket at helpdesk.nexustech.in.

Q: Are tips reimbursable?
A: Customary tips during client entertainment (up to 10% of bill) are
   reimbursable. Include in the total bill amount.
