1. BRAND OVERVIEW & MISSION
================================================================================

About NexusTech Solutions
NexusTech Solutions is a mid-size technology company headquartered in Bangalore,
India, with offices in Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Chennai. Founded in 2015, we build
enterprise software products and cloud solutions for clients in BFSI, healthcare,
logistics, and retail sectors across India and Southeast Asia.

Our Mission:
"Empowering enterprises with intelligent, scalable, and human-centric technology
solutions that turn complexity into competitive advantage."

Our Vision:
"To be the most trusted technology partner for mid-market enterprises in
South and Southeast Asia by 2030."

Our Core Values:
  1. Integrity First — We are transparent, honest, and accountable.
  2. Customer Obsession — Our clients' success is our success.
  3. Engineering Excellence — We build things that last.
  4. Inclusive Innovation — Great ideas come from everywhere.
  5. Radical Ownership — We don't blame; we solve.

Brand Positioning Statement:
NexusTech Solutions stands for dependable, sophisticated technology — the kind
that enterprises trust with their most critical operations. We are not a startup
chasing hype; we are a reliable partner building long-term relationships through
consistent delivery and deep domain expertise.

Target Audience:
  Primary: CTOs, CIOs, and IT Decision Makers in mid-market enterprises
           (500–5,000 employees) in BFSI, Healthcare, and Logistics.
  Secondary: Engineering leads, Product managers, and Procurement teams.
  Tertiary: Developers and technical evaluators conducting PoC assessments.

2. BRAND PERSONALITY & VOICE
================================================================================

If NexusTech Solutions were a person, they would be:
  "A seasoned senior engineer who has become a trusted advisor to business
  leaders — technically brilliant, calm under pressure, straight-talking,
  and genuinely invested in helping clients succeed. Not flashy, not
  buzzword-heavy, but deeply credible and always reliable."

Brand Personality Attributes:
  - Authoritative but Approachable
  - Technically Rigorous but Human
  - Confident but Not Arrogant
  - Forward-Looking but Grounded
  - Precise but not Pedantic

What we are NOT:
  - We are NOT a hyped-up startup using buzzwords to compensate for substance.
  - We are NOT overly casual or jokey in B2B contexts.
  - We are NOT stiff, robotic, or overly formal in a way that feels detached.
  - We are NOT vague or fluffy ("leveraging synergies," "holistic transformation").

3. TONE OF VOICE GUIDELINES
================================================================================

3.1 Core Tone Dimensions

  AUTHORITATIVE (not arrogant):
    Write from a position of expertise. Make claims we can back up.
    ✓ "NexusTech's supply chain platform reduces order processing time by 40%
       on average across our 80+ enterprise deployments."
    ✗ "NexusTech is the market leader in supply chain technology."

  CLEAR (not jargon-heavy):
    Use plain language. Explain technical concepts simply.
    ✓ "Our API handles 50,000 requests per second with 99.99% uptime."
    ✗ "Our hyper-scalable, AI-native, cloud-agnostic microservices architecture
       delivers frictionless digital transformation."

  HUMAN (not robotic):
    Write as one human to another. Acknowledge challenges, show empathy.
    ✓ "We know migrating legacy systems is stressful. We've guided 60+
       enterprises through it — here's what we've learned."
    ✗ "NexusTech Solutions offers legacy modernization services."

  CONFIDENT (not pushy):
    Describe our capabilities firmly; don't beg or oversell.
    ✓ "Book a demo to see our platform in action."
    ✗ "Please give us a chance to show you! You won't be disappointed!!!"

  SPECIFIC (not vague):
    Use numbers, examples, and real outcomes wherever possible.
    ✓ "Deployed in 9 months. Zero production downtime since launch."
    ✗ "We deliver fast, high-quality projects."

3.2 Tone by Channel

  Website / Product Pages:
    Confident, benefit-led, specific. Lead with outcomes, not features.
    "Your finance team closes 3x faster. Our reconciliation engine makes it
    possible."

  Case Studies / Whitepapers:
    Authoritative, data-driven, narrative-led. Tell the client's story.
    Let measurable outcomes speak. Avoid self-congratulatory language.

  LinkedIn / Social Media:
    Conversational but professional. Educational and thought-leadership
    oriented. Engagement > promotion.
    Mix: 60% thought leadership, 20% product/company news, 20% culture/team.

  Email (Marketing):
    Personalized, clear subject lines, short paragraphs, single clear CTA.
    Never use all-caps subject lines. Never use "RE:" falsely.

  Press Releases:
    Formal AP Style. Third-person references only. Include quotes from
    named spokespeople (CEO, VP Marketing, or client contact).

  Sales Collateral (Pitch Decks, One-Pagers):
    Crisp, punchy, visual. Use the client's language / industry terms.
    Lead with their pain, then our solution, then proof.

  Internal Communications:
    Direct and human. Assume intelligence. Respect people's time.
    No corporate fluff; get to the point.

4. LOGO USAGE RULES
================================================================================

4.1 Primary Logo
  The NexusTech Solutions logo consists of:
    - The "Nexus N" mark (geometric, interlocking node symbol in brand blue)
    - The wordmark "NexusTech Solutions" in Graphik Semibold

  Approved logo files are located in the Brand Asset Library:
    Confluence: confluence.nexustech.in/brand/assets/logos
    Formats available: SVG, PNG (transparent), PDF, EPS

4.2 Logo Variants
  Full Lockup (horizontal): Default for most uses.
  Stacked Lockup: For square/compact spaces (app icons, social avatars).
  Symbol Only ("N" mark): Only for contexts where the brand is already
  established (social media profile icons, favicons, badges, watermarks).
  Wordmark Only: Do not use without special approval from Marketing.

4.3 Logo Clearance Zone
  Minimum clear space around the logo = Height of the "N" symbol on all sides.
  Never place text, icons, or imagery within this clearance zone.

4.4 Minimum Size
  Print: Minimum 25mm wide for full lockup; 8mm for symbol only.
  Digital: Minimum 120px wide for full lockup; 32px for symbol only.

4.5 Prohibited Logo Modifications
  - Do NOT stretch, skew, or rotate the logo.
  - Do NOT change the logo colors.
  - Do NOT add drop shadows, gradients, or outlines to the logo.
  - Do NOT place the logo on a busy background without a clear background
    panel.
  - Do NOT recreate the logo in a different font or style.
  - Do NOT use old versions of the logo (pre-2023 refresh).

4.6 Logo on Backgrounds
  On white/light backgrounds: Use full-color logo.
  On dark/brand-blue backgrounds: Use white reversed logo.
  On photographs: Use white logo with subtle drop shadow, or place over a
  semi-transparent dark overlay.

5. COLOR PALETTE
================================================================================

PRIMARY COLORS
  Brand Blue (Primary)
    HEX: #1A4FCC    RGB: 26, 79, 204    CMYK: 87, 61, 0, 20
    Pantone: 2728 C
    Usage: Primary CTAs, headings, logo, key data points.

  Midnight Navy (Dark)
    HEX: #0D1F4D    RGB: 13, 31, 77     CMYK: 100, 80, 0, 45
    Usage: Dark backgrounds, footers, premium/enterprise contexts.

  Clean White
    HEX: #FFFFFF    RGB: 255, 255, 255
    Usage: Backgrounds, reverse text on dark.

SECONDARY COLORS
  Electric Teal (Accent)
    HEX: #00C8C0    RGB: 0, 200, 192
    Usage: Data visualizations, highlights, badges, hover states. Use sparingly.

  Warm Slate (Neutral)
    HEX: #F4F6FA    RGB: 244, 246, 250
    Usage: Card backgrounds, section dividers, subtle UI elements.

  Carbon Grey
    HEX: #3D3D3D    RGB: 61, 61, 61
    Usage: Body text on white backgrounds.

STATUS / FUNCTIONAL COLORS
  Success Green : #18A34A
  Warning Amber : #F59E0B
  Error Red     : #DC2626
  Info Blue     : #2563EB

USAGE RULES
  - Brand Blue is the dominant color; use in ≥60% of color application.
  - Electric Teal is an accent — use for emphasis, not as background.
  - Maintain AA accessibility standards for text contrast ratios
    (minimum 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text).

6. TYPOGRAPHY
================================================================================

PRIMARY TYPEFACE: Graphik (licensed via Klim Type Foundry)
  Graphik Semibold  : H1, H2 headings, navigation, logo wordmark
  Graphik Medium    : H3, H4 subheadings, CTA buttons, callouts
  Graphik Regular   : Body text, UI labels, captions

SECONDARY TYPEFACE: Inter (Google Fonts — free)
  Inter Medium      : UI interfaces, dashboards, data-heavy contexts
  Inter Regular     : UI body text, form labels
  Usage: When Graphik is unavailable (e.g., Google Docs, Notion).

CODE / MONOSPACE: JetBrains Mono
  Usage: Code samples, terminal outputs, technical documentation.

SYSTEM FALLBACK (email, presentations if fonts unavailable):
  Arial / Helvetica Neue (never Comic Sans, Times New Roman, or Papyrus)

TYPE SCALE (Web):
  H1: 48px / 52px line height / Graphik Semibold
  H2: 36px / 42px / Graphik Semibold
  H3: 28px / 36px / Graphik Medium
  H4: 22px / 30px / Graphik Medium
  Body Large: 18px / 28px / Graphik Regular
  Body: 16px / 26px / Graphik Regular
  Small / Caption: 14px / 22px / Graphik Regular

  Letter spacing: -0.02em for headings; 0 for body.

7. IMAGERY & PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE
================================================================================

7.1 Style Direction
  Photography and imagery at NexusTech should feel:
    - Real and authentic (not stock-photo-generic)
    - Technically sophisticated (clean environments, purposeful setups)
    - Diverse and inclusive (reflect the teams we are and the clients we serve)
    - Optimistic and progressive (forward-looking, not nostalgic)

7.2 People Photography
  DO:
    - Show real NexusTech employees (with consent) or diverse stock talent.
    - Show genuine interactions: collaboration, problem-solving, focused work.
    - Include diverse representation: gender, ethnicity, age, ability.
  DON'T:
    - Overly staged, fake-smiling "stock photo" poses.
    - All-male engineering teams or homogenous groups.
    - Outdated office aesthetics (cubicle farms, old monitors).

7.3 Technology / Product Photography
  DO:
    - Clean, minimal, high-contrast product screens on modern hardware.
    - Data visualizations, dashboards, and interfaces should look real/polished.
    - Dark-mode UI photography for premium/enterprise contexts.
  DON'T:
    - Blurry or pixelated screenshots.
    - Showing competitor products.
    - Generic "lock and shield" cybersecurity stock images.

7.4 Color Treatment
  Apply Brand Blue or Midnight Navy tints to photography when used in headers
  or hero sections. Overlay opacity: 60–75% for readability.

7.5 Approved Stock Sources
  Getty Images (corporate account: mkt-design@nexustech.in)
  Unsplash (free; filter for commercial use)
  Pexels (free; filter for commercial use)
  Do NOT use images from Google Image Search directly.

9. WRITING STYLE GUIDE
================================================================================

9.1 General Rules
  - Use Oxford comma: "data, privacy, and security" not "data, privacy and
    security."
  - Use active voice: "We built X" not "X was built by us."
  - Spell out numbers one through nine; use numerals for 10 and above.
  - Use % (not "percent") with numerals: "40%", "8%".
  - Dates: Use "January 15, 2026" (not "15th January" or "1/15/26").
  - Times: "2:00 PM IST" (use 12-hour format with AM/PM; include timezone).
  - Currency: "INR 5,000" or "₹5,000" for Indian rupee; "USD 2,000" for US
    dollars.
  - Em dash: Use — (em dash) not - (hyphen) for parenthetical clauses.
    No spaces around em dash in headlines; one space on each side in body.

9.2 Product & Feature Naming
  Capitalize product names: NexusTech FinOS, NexusTech LogiTrack,
  NexusTech CloudDeploy.
  Lowercase descriptive terms: "dashboard," "API," "reporting module."
  Never use casual abbreviations in external communication:
    ✓ "NexusTech FinOS"    ✗ "finOS" or "the finance thing"

9.3 Preferred Terminology
  SAY                         | AVOID
  ----------------------------|------------------------
  Enterprise clients          | Customers (in B2B context)
  Platform                    | Tool (minimizes perceived value)
  Engineering team            | Dev team (in external docs)
  Deployment                  | Launch (for technical context)
  Onboarding                  | Training (implies difficulty)
  Partnership                 | Vendor relationship
  Scalable                    | Only if we can prove it with data
  AI-powered                  | AI-enabled, AI-assisted (be specific)
  Cloud-native                | SaaS (if not strictly SaaS)

9.4 Inclusive Language
  Use gender-neutral language in all communications:
    "they/them" as singular pronoun when gender unknown.
    "team" or "staff" instead of "guys."
    "chair" instead of "chairman."
    "workforce" instead of "manpower."

14. DO'S AND DON'TS
================================================================================

DO:
  ✓ Lead with client outcomes and specific numbers.
  ✓ Use real employee and client quotes (with permission).
  ✓ Write in active voice.
  ✓ Maintain consistent terminology across all documents.
  ✓ Proofread everything before publishing.
  ✓ Get Legal review for any content making legal/compliance claims.
  ✓ Attribute sources for third-party statistics.

DON'T:
  ✗ Use "disruptive," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "synergize," or
    "leverage" as generic qualifiers.
  ✗ Make performance claims without supporting data.
  ✗ Reference competitor names negatively.
  ✗ Use Comic Sans, WordArt, or non-brand fonts anywhere.
  ✗ Share client names or logos without a signed logo permission form.
  ✗ Post company news on personal social media before official announcement.
  ✗ Use ALL CAPS for emphasis (use **bold** in markdown, weight in design).
