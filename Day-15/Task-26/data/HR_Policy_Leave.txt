1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

1.1 Purpose
This policy establishes consistent, fair, and transparent leave management
practices at NexusTech Solutions. It ensures employees can maintain work-life
balance while meeting business commitments.

1.2 Scope
This policy applies to all full-time permanent employees of NexusTech Solutions
across all office locations: Bangalore HQ, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Chennai, and
remote employees based in India. Contract staff, interns, and consultants are
governed by separate agreements unless explicitly referenced herein.

1.3 Guiding Principles
- All leave types are a right, not a privilege.
- Employees must not be penalized for taking legitimate leave.
- Managers must handle leave requests with empathy and fairness.
- Leave records are confidential and stored securely in the HRMS.

2. TYPES OF LEAVE
================================================================================

2.1 ANNUAL / VACATION LEAVE
-----------------------------
Entitlement:
  - Employees with 0–2 years of service   : 15 days per calendar year
  - Employees with 2–5 years of service   : 18 days per calendar year
  - Employees with 5+ years of service    : 21 days per calendar year

Accrual: Leave accrues at the start of each quarter (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct) in
equal installments (e.g., 15 days/year = 3.75 days per quarter).

Minimum Notice: Employees must apply for vacation leave at least 5 working days
in advance for durations of 1–3 days, and at least 15 working days in advance
for durations of 4 days or more.

Approval: Subject to manager approval and team capacity. Approval must be
communicated within 3 working days of request submission.

Restrictions:
  - Maximum 10 consecutive vacation days permitted without VP approval.
  - Cannot be taken during critical project delivery milestones (blackout
    periods declared by the respective department head).
  - Cannot be clubbed with more than 2 public holidays without manager approval.

Encashment:
  - Employees may encash up to 5 unused vacation days at the end of a calendar
    year, at their current basic salary rate.
  - Encashment requests must be submitted by December 15 each year.
  - Encashment is not available during notice period.

2.2 SICK LEAVE
--------------
Entitlement: 12 days per calendar year (non-cumulative).

Usage:
  - For illness, injury, medical procedures, hospitalization, or attending to
    a sick immediate family member (spouse, children, parents).
  - No advance notice required; employee must notify their direct manager and
    HR before 10:00 AM on the first day of absence via HRMS or Slack.

Medical Certificate:
  - For illness lasting 3 or more consecutive working days, a medical
    certificate from a registered medical practitioner must be submitted within
    3 working days of returning to work.
  - Failure to provide documentation may result in the leave being converted
    to Loss of Pay (LOP).

Carry-Forward: Sick leave does not carry forward to the next calendar year.
Unused sick leave lapses at year-end and cannot be encashed.

2.3 CASUAL LEAVE
----------------
Entitlement: 6 days per calendar year.
Purpose: Short, unplanned personal needs (household emergencies, civic duties,
personal appointments).
Notice: Minimum 1 day prior notice preferred; same-day notice acceptable for
emergencies.
Restrictions:
  - Maximum 2 consecutive casual leave days.
  - Cannot be clubbed with vacation leave.
  - Unused casual leave does not carry forward and cannot be encashed.

2.4 MATERNITY LEAVE
-------------------
Entitlement: 26 weeks (182 days) of paid maternity leave as per the Maternity
Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017 for the first two children.
For the third child onwards: 12 weeks paid maternity leave.

Extended Leave: Additional 4 weeks of unpaid leave may be granted post-delivery
with prior HR approval.

Adoption: Female employees who adopt a child below 3 months of age are entitled
to 12 weeks of paid maternity leave.

Commissioning Mothers: Biological mothers in surrogacy arrangements are
entitled to 12 weeks of paid leave from the date the child is handed over.

Work-from-Home Post Return: On return from maternity leave, employees may
request a 4-week WFH period without loss of pay.

Documents Required:
  - Expected date of delivery certificate from a gynecologist.
  - Birth certificate (within 30 days of return).

2.5 PATERNITY LEAVE
-------------------
Entitlement: 10 working days paid paternity leave.
Eligibility: All male employees and partners in same-sex relationships for
birth or legal adoption.
Timing: Must be availed within 90 days of the child's birth or adoption date.
Cannot be split into more than 2 separate periods.

2.6 BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
---------------------
Entitlement:
  - Death of spouse, child, parent, or sibling            : 5 working days
  - Death of in-laws (mother-in-law / father-in-law)      : 3 working days
  - Death of grandparent, uncle, aunt, first cousin       : 2 working days

Extension: One additional day per 500 km distance for travel to attend last
rites (maximum 2 extra days).
Documentation: An obituary, death certificate, or funeral program may be
requested in exceptional cases.

2.7 MARRIAGE LEAVE
------------------
Entitlement: 5 working days paid leave for the employee's own marriage.
Eligibility: Applicable once during the entire tenure of employment.
Notice: Minimum 15 days prior notice required.

2.8 COMPENSATORY OFF (COMP-OFF)
--------------------------------
Applicability: When an employee is required to work on a Sunday, public
holiday, or declared company holiday due to business needs.
Accrual: 1 comp-off day for every day worked on an off-day (minimum 4 hours
constitutes half a comp-off).
Validity: Comp-off must be availed within 60 days of accrual. Expired
comp-offs lapse and cannot be encashed.
Process: Manager must raise a comp-off request in HRMS within 2 days of the
employee working on a holiday.

2.9 UNPAID LEAVE (LOSS OF PAY — LOP)
--------------------------------------
Granted when all paid leave balances are exhausted.
Requires manager + HR approval.
Salary is deducted on a pro-rata basis for each LOP day.
Continuous LOP beyond 30 days without prior approval is treated as voluntary
abandonment of employment.

2.10 STUDY / EXAM LEAVE
------------------------
Entitlement: Up to 5 days per calendar year for employees pursuing
company-sponsored certifications or approved higher education programs.
Proof of exam enrollment and results must be submitted to HR within 30 days.

2.11 EMERGENCY LEAVE
--------------------
Up to 2 additional days per year may be approved by the HR Director for
genuine emergencies not covered by other leave types (natural disasters, civil
unrest, medical emergencies of dependents).
These are granted at HR's sole discretion on a case-by-case basis.

4. LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS
================================================================================

Step 1 — Apply via HRMS:
  Log in to portal.nexustech.in → My Leave → Apply Leave → Select leave type,
  dates, and add a reason. System auto-checks balance and flags conflicts.

Step 2 — Manager Notification:
  System auto-emails the direct manager and creates a task in the manager's
  dashboard. For leaves >5 days, the skip-level manager is also notified.

Step 3 — Manager Action:
  Manager must approve or reject within:
    - 1 working day for urgent/same-day requests
    - 3 working days for planned requests
  If no action taken within 3 days, the system escalates to the department head.

Step 4 — Confirmation:
  Employee receives email confirmation with updated leave balance.

Verbal/Informal Approvals: Not valid. All leave must be formally applied and
approved in HRMS. Verbal approvals from managers do not constitute official
leave sanction.

Backdating: Backdating leave applications is not permitted beyond 5 working
days. Beyond this, approval from HR Director is required.

================================================================================
5. LEAVE CANCELLATION & RECALL
================================================================================

Cancellation by Employee:
  Employees may cancel approved leave at least 2 working days before the start
  date via HRMS. The balance is restored automatically.

Recall from Leave:
  In exceptional business circumstances, a manager may recall an employee from
  approved leave. In such cases:
    - Employee will receive a comp-off for unused leave days recalled.
    - Travel costs incurred due to the recall are reimbursed with manager and
      Finance approval.
    - Employee has the right to decline recall from maternity/bereavement leave.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
================================================================================

NexusTech Solutions observes 10 mandatory national public holidays and provides
an additional 5 optional "floating holidays" that employees may use at their
discretion. The official holiday calendar is published by HR in December of the
preceding year for all office locations.

Mandatory Holidays (National): Republic Day, Holi, Good Friday, Independence
Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Dussehra, Diwali, Eid ul-Fitr, Christmas, and one
regional holiday per office location.

Optional/Floating Holidays: Employees select 5 from a list of ~12 regional and
religious festivals. Must be declared in HRMS by January 31 each year.
Unused floating holidays lapse at year-end and cannot be encashed.

7. HALF-DAY LEAVE
================================================================================

Half-day leave = 0.5 leave day deducted from the applicable leave balance.
First half: 9:30 AM – 1:30 PM
Second half: 1:30 PM – 6:30 PM
Cannot be combined with flexi-start/end without manager approval.

================================================================================
8. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP) GUIDELINES
================================================================================

LWP is permitted under extraordinary personal circumstances (severe illness,
caring for a critically ill dependent, personal sabbatical) after all paid
leave is exhausted.
Maximum continuous LWP: 90 days per year (requires CHRO approval beyond 30).
Benefits: Health insurance and PF contributions continue during LWP.
Probation & Confirmation: Leave taken during probation does not count toward
LWP limits; however, excessive leaves during probation may delay confirmation.

================================================================================
9. RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

Employee Responsibilities:
  - Apply leave proactively and with adequate notice.
  - Keep manager informed of medical situations without disclosing confidential
    health details unnecessarily.
  - Return to work on the committed date; inform HR if extension is needed.

Manager Responsibilities:
  - Respond to leave requests within the stipulated time.
  - Ensure team coverage plans are in place for extended absences.
  - Not discourage employees from taking legitimate leave.
  - Maintain confidentiality regarding the nature of employee leave.

HR Responsibilities:
  - Maintain accurate leave records in HRMS.
  - Publish holiday calendars annually.
  - Audit leave balances quarterly.
  - Handle disputes and escalations.

================================================================================
10. NON-COMPLIANCE & DISCIPLINARY ACTION
================================================================================

- Misrepresentation of leave reasons: Written warning → salary deduction →
  termination depending on severity and recurrence.
- Overstaying approved leave without communication: Leave converted to LOP;
  second occurrence triggers disciplinary process.
- Managers delaying leave approvals deliberately: Escalated to HR; performance
  note recorded.

11. FAQs
================================================================================

Q: Can I apply for leave on the same day?
A: Sick and casual leave can be applied on the day itself; vacation leave
   requires advance notice.

Q: Can leave be taken during the notice period?
A: Casual and sick leave may be taken during notice. Vacation leave during
   notice requires mutual agreement. Notice period is not extended for
   leaves taken; salary is adjusted instead.

Q: What happens to leave balance when I am promoted or change teams?
A: Leave balances are not affected by internal movements.

Q: Can I split my maternity leave?
A: Maternity leave cannot be split; it must be taken as a continuous block
   starting at most 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

Q: Is there a minimum service period before I can take vacation leave?
A: Employees must complete 3 months of service to avail vacation leave.
   Sick and casual leave are available from day 1.

Q: Who do I contact for HRMS technical issues?
A: Raise a ticket at helpdesk.nexustech.in or email hrtech@nexustech.in.
