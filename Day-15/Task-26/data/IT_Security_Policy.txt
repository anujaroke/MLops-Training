1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

1.1 Purpose
NexusTech Solutions handles sensitive customer data, intellectual property, and
business-critical information. This IT Security Policy establishes the baseline
security controls, practices, and responsibilities required to protect company
and customer assets from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or
destruction.

1.2 Scope
This policy applies to:
  - All full-time employees, part-time employees, and interns.
  - All contractors, vendors, and third parties accessing NexusTech systems.
  - All NexusTech-owned, leased, and personally-owned (BYOD) devices used for
    company work.
  - All locations: Bangalore HQ, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Chennai, and remote setups.

1.3 Regulatory Alignment
This policy aligns with:
  - ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management Standard
  - India's Information Technology Act, 2000 (and amendments)
  - GDPR (for EU customer data handling)
  - SOC 2 Type II requirements (applicable to product engineering teams)

2. INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION
================================================================================

All NexusTech information assets must be classified into one of the following
four tiers:

TIER 1 — PUBLIC
  Definition: Information approved for public release (press releases, public
  documentation, marketing materials).
  Handling: No restrictions.
  Examples: Website content, job postings, published blog articles.

TIER 2 — INTERNAL
  Definition: Information for employee use that is not sensitive but not meant
  for public consumption.
  Handling: Do not share outside the company without authorization.
  Examples: Internal memos, team announcements, training materials.

TIER 3 — CONFIDENTIAL
  Definition: Sensitive business information that could harm the company if
  disclosed.
  Handling: Share only on a need-to-know basis; encrypt when transmitting;
  do not store on personal devices.
  Examples: Source code, financial reports, product roadmaps, customer lists,
  employee salary data, vendor contracts.

TIER 4 — RESTRICTED / SECRET
  Definition: Highest sensitivity; unauthorized disclosure could cause severe
  legal, financial, or reputational damage.
  Handling: Access strictly controlled via IAM; encrypted at rest and in
  transit; physical documents in locked cabinets; audit logs mandatory.
  Examples: Customer PII, payment card data, M&A negotiations, security keys,
  authentication credentials, penetration test reports.

Classification Labels: All digital documents must carry classification in the
document header/footer. All emails containing Tier 3 or 4 data must include
"[CONFIDENTIAL]" or "[RESTRICTED]" in the subject line.

3. ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
================================================================================

3.1 Permitted Uses
  - Work-related communications, research, and development activities.
  - Reasonable personal use that does not interfere with productivity or
    violate any other provision of this policy.
  - Using company-approved tools and platforms listed in the IT-approved
    software catalog.

3.2 Prohibited Activities
The following are strictly prohibited on any NexusTech system or network:

  a) Accessing, downloading, or distributing pornographic, hateful, or
     otherwise offensive content.
  b) Installing unauthorized software, browser extensions, or scripts.
  c) Attempting to access systems, accounts, or data without authorization.
  d) Using company resources for personal commercial activities, crypto-
     currency mining, or running personal servers.
  e) Sharing credentials (passwords, API keys, SSH keys) with any other
     individual, including teammates.
  f) Disabling security tools such as antivirus, EDR agents, or DLP software.
  g) Connecting to corporate VPN from public/unsecured Wi-Fi without using an
     additional VPN or hotspot.
  h) Storing company data (Tier 3 or 4) on personal cloud storage (Google
     Drive personal, Dropbox personal, iCloud, etc.).
  i) Using unauthorized AI tools to process confidential company or customer
     data (e.g., pasting Tier 3/4 data into public LLM chatbots).
  j) Circumventing IT controls, firewalls, or content filters.

4. PASSWORD & AUTHENTICATION POLICY
================================================================================

4.1 Password Standards
  Minimum length       : 14 characters
  Complexity           : Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and
                         at least 2 special characters (!@#$%^&* etc.)
  Prohibited passwords : Dictionary words, keyboard sequences (qwerty, 12345),
                         personal information (name, DOB, employee ID),
                         previous 12 passwords.
  Maximum age          : 90 days for standard accounts; 30 days for privileged/
                         admin accounts.
  Password reuse       : Last 12 passwords cannot be reused.

4.2 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
  Mandatory for: All corporate SSO logins, VPN access, cloud consoles (AWS,
  GCP, Azure), code repositories (GitHub Enterprise), production server access,
  HR and Finance systems.
  Acceptable MFA methods (in order of preference):
    1. Hardware security key (YubiKey — company-issued for engineers)
    2. Authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator)
    3. SMS OTP (acceptable but discouraged for privileged accounts due to
       SIM-swap risk)
  MFA bypass codes: Must be stored in company-approved password manager
  (1Password Teams), not in personal notes or email.

4.3 Password Manager
  All employees must use 1Password Teams for storing work-related credentials.
  Sharing credentials via Slack, email, or any messaging platform is prohibited.
  Use 1Password's "Share" feature for temporary secure credential sharing.

4.4 Service Accounts & API Keys
  API keys and service account credentials must:
    - Be stored in HashiCorp Vault or AWS Secrets Manager.
    - Never be hardcoded in source code, config files, or commit history.
    - Be rotated every 60 days or immediately upon suspected compromise.
    - Follow least-privilege principle (minimum permissions required).

5. DEVICE & ENDPOINT SECURITY
================================================================================

5.1 Company-Issued Devices
  All NexusTech-issued laptops, desktops, and mobile devices must:
  - Run the latest approved OS version (auto-updates enabled).
  - Have full-disk encryption enabled (BitLocker for Windows, FileVault for
    macOS, LUKS for Linux).
  - Have Crowdstrike Falcon EDR agent installed and running at all times.
  - Be enrolled in Mobile Device Management (MDM) — Jamf for macOS,
    Intune for Windows.
  - Have screen lock set to activate after 5 minutes of inactivity.
  - Require PIN/password/biometric to unlock.
  - Have the NexusTech VPN (WireGuard) client installed.

5.2 BYOD (Bring Your Own Device)
  Personal devices may be used for work email and Slack only, under these
  conditions:
  - Device must run a supported OS (Android 12+, iOS 16+).
  - Company apps must be installed via the managed app catalog.
  - IT may perform a remote wipe of company data from the device if the device
    is reported lost, stolen, or the employee leaves the company.
  - Employees must accept the BYOD agreement before enrollment.
  BYOD devices cannot access code repositories, production systems, or any
  Tier 3/4 data.

5.3 Device Loss or Theft
  Report immediately to IT Security at security@nexustech.in or call the
  Security Hotline: +91-80-4567-8901 (available 24x7).
  IT will immediately revoke all sessions and initiate remote wipe.
  Employee must file an FIR if the device is stolen.

5.4 End-of-Life Devices
  Devices retired from service must be sanitized by IT using DoD 5220.22-M
  standard (7-pass wipe) or physical destruction for SSDs before disposal.

6. NETWORK SECURITY
================================================================================

6.1 VPN Policy
  All employees working remotely must connect to the NexusTech corporate VPN
  before accessing any internal systems.
  VPN credentials are unique per employee; sharing is prohibited.
  VPN access is logged and audited.

6.2 Office Network
  Company Wi-Fi networks are segmented:
    - NexusTech-Corp   : Employee devices (requires MDM enrollment)
    - NexusTech-Guest  : Visitors / personal devices (internet only)
    - NexusTech-IoT    : Conference room devices, printers (isolated)
  Connecting personal devices to NexusTech-Corp is prohibited.

6.3 Firewall & DNS
  Outbound internet access is filtered via Zscaler Web Proxy.
  DNS requests are resolved via company-controlled DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH).
  Employees must not change DNS settings on company devices.

6.4 Port & Protocol Controls
  Only approved ports and protocols may be used for internal services.
  Opening firewall rules requires a change request approved by the Security team.

7. DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY
================================================================================

7.1 Data Storage
  All Tier 3/4 data must be stored on NexusTech-managed storage systems
  (SharePoint, AWS S3 with SSE, or company-approved databases).
  Storing Tier 3/4 data on personal devices or unapproved cloud services is
  strictly prohibited.

7.2 Data Transmission
  All data in transit must be encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  Internal file transfers must use SFTP or company-managed file-sharing tools.
  USB drives are prohibited for transferring Tier 3/4 data. If required,
  encrypted USB devices may be used with prior IT approval.

7.3 Data Retention & Disposal
  Data Retention periods:
    - Customer transaction data    : 7 years
    - Employee records             : 7 years post employment
    - Security logs                : 1 year online, 3 years archived
    - Marketing campaign data      : 3 years
  Data past retention period must be disposed of per the Data Disposal SOP
  (IT-SOP-DataDisposal-007).

7.4 Customer Data Handling
  Customer PII must never be used in non-production environments (dev/staging)
  unless anonymized or synthetically generated.
  Any employee accessing customer data must have a legitimate business reason
  and such access is logged and auditable.

8. EMAIL & COMMUNICATION SECURITY
================================================================================

8.1 Email
  Company email (Google Workspace) is the official communication channel.
  Phishing awareness: Employees must not click links or download attachments in
  suspicious emails. Report suspicious emails via the "Report Phishing" button
  in Gmail or forward to phishing@nexustech.in.
  Do not send Tier 3/4 data via email unless encrypted with S/MIME or using
  Google Confidential Mode.
  Auto-forward of work emails to personal accounts is disabled and prohibited.

8.2 Slack
  Slack is used for internal team communication.
  Slack messages are subject to the same data classification rules.
  Do not share credentials, API keys, or sensitive customer data in Slack,
  even in private channels.
  #incident-response and #security-alerts are monitored channels.

8.3 Video Conferencing
  Use only approved platforms: Google Meet or Zoom (company account).
  Do not record calls containing Tier 3/4 information without explicit consent
  of all participants.
  Waiting rooms must be enabled for external meetings.

9. CLOUD & SaaS USAGE
================================================================================

Approved cloud platforms: AWS (primary), GCP (secondary), Azure (HR systems).
All cloud resources must be provisioned via the Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC)
pipeline; manual provisioning in production is prohibited without Change Mgmt.

Approved SaaS tools (as of Q1 2026):
  Productivity    : Google Workspace, Notion, Confluence
  Communication   : Slack, Zoom, Google Meet
  Engineering     : GitHub Enterprise, Jira, Datadog, PagerDuty, Sentry
  HR & Finance    : Darwinbox (HRMS), Zoho Books, Expensify
  Security        : 1Password Teams, Crowdstrike, Zscaler, HashiCorp Vault
  AI Tools        : GitHub Copilot (approved), Gemini for Workspace (approved)

Shadow IT: Using any tool not on the approved list with company data requires
written approval from IT Security and Legal. Submit requests to
it-approvals@nexustech.in.

10. SOFTWARE & APPLICATION POLICY
================================================================================

Only software from the IT-approved catalog may be installed on company devices.
Employees may request new software via the IT portal (helpdesk.nexustech.in).

Open-source software used in products must be reviewed for license compatibility
(GPL, LGPL, MIT, Apache) by the Legal team before inclusion.

Software license compliance: IT tracks licenses via Flexera. Employees must not
install software for which NexusTech does not hold a license.

Browser extensions: Only extensions from the IT-approved list are permitted on
company devices. Unapproved extensions must be removed.

11. INCIDENT REPORTING & RESPONSE
================================================================================

11.1 What to Report
  - Lost or stolen devices
  - Suspected phishing or social engineering attempts
  - Unauthorized access to systems or data
  - Accidental exposure of Tier 3/4 data (including sending to wrong recipient)
  - Malware alerts or unusual system behavior
  - Discovery of hardcoded secrets in code

11.2 How to Report
  Email    : security@nexustech.in
  Slack    : #security-incidents channel (do not share details publicly)
  Hotline  : +91-80-4567-8901 (24x7)
  Portal   : helpdesk.nexustech.in → Security Incident

11.3 Response Timeline (by Severity)
  Critical (data breach, ransomware, production compromise):
    - Detection to response: < 1 hour
    - CISO and CTO notified immediately
    - Customer notification per legal obligation within 72 hours (GDPR)
  High (unauthorized access, privilege escalation):
    - Response: < 4 hours
  Medium (phishing attempt, policy violation):
    - Response: < 24 hours
  Low (minor configuration issue, informational):
    - Response: < 72 hours

11.4 Post-Incident
  All Critical and High incidents require a post-mortem document within 5
  business days. Post-mortems are blameless and focus on systemic improvement.

12. PHYSICAL SECURITY
================================================================================

- Office entry requires badge access for all employees and visitors.
- Visitors must sign in at reception and wear visitor badges at all times.
- Server rooms require two-factor physical access (badge + PIN).
- Clean desk policy: Confidential physical documents must be locked away.
  Workstations must be locked when unattended.
- No photography or recording in server rooms or R&D labs.
- Tailgating/piggybacking through secured doors is prohibited.

================================================================================
13. THIRD-PARTY & VENDOR ACCESS
================================================================================

- Vendors requiring access to NexusTech systems must sign an NDA and the
  Vendor Security Addendum before access is provisioned.
- Third-party access must follow least-privilege principles and be time-bound.
- All vendor access is logged and reviewed quarterly.
- Vendors accessing Tier 4 data must complete a security assessment by NexusTech
  Security team.

================================================================================
14. SECURITY AWARENESS & TRAINING
================================================================================

- All employees must complete the Annual Security Awareness Training (2 hours)
  within 30 days of joining and every January thereafter.
- Engineering teams must complete the Secure Coding Fundamentals course annually.
- NexusTech conducts quarterly phishing simulations; employees who fail 2
  consecutive simulations must attend remedial training.
- The Security team publishes a monthly "Security Bulletin" newsletter.

15. CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

Violation Severity | Consequence
-------------------|-------------------------------------------------------
Minor              | Verbal warning; re-training required
Moderate           | Written warning; access temporarily restricted
Serious            | Formal disciplinary action; potential role change
Critical           | Immediate suspension; potential termination; legal action
                   | (applies to willful data exfiltration, sabotage, fraud)

NexusTech reserves the right to monitor all company systems and devices for
compliance with this policy. Monitoring is disclosed to employees upon onboarding.
